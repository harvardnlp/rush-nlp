I'm an ML researcher, programmer, and blogger. I was previously a professor at Cornell and Harvard, where my group's research was on early LLMs, NLP, and infrastructure. I worked as an ML researcher at Cursor, where I helped develop the Composer series of models, and Hugging Face, where we built open-source LLMs. I helped start the Conference on Language Modeling and was a General Chair of ICLR.
